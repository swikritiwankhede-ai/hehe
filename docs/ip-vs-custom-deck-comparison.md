# IP vs Custom: what each deck actually contains, and where it comes from

This compares the **Brand World Summit** IP deck with the **Lenovo Leaders Circle '26** Custom deck. The BWS figures are the 2024 actuals, as reported in the BWS 2024 post-event report and reused in the BWS 2025 sales deck. Details: `docs/ip-post-event-deck-sources.md`, `docs/bws-2025-deck-retention-gaps.md`, `docs/custom-post-event-deck-sources.md`.

**The difference in one line:** an IP runs on an agenda ET sets for many partners, so its deck proves **the event's scale and reach**. A Custom is designed around one sponsor's brief, so its deck proves **that sponsor's commitments were delivered**. **Wishlist-met is not an IP deck data point.** It only appears where a partner bought an access add-on (Pitch Room, Leadership Dialogues, Customer Success Showcase), and even then it isn't measured in today's BWS deck.

## What this means for the automated reports
- **Both decks share:**
  - event identity
  - an audience classifier (seniority, organisation type)
  - speakers and sessions
  - photos and quotes

  The same platform pipeline serves both.
- **IP only:**
  - scale numbers
  - the partner wall by tier
  - social, video, OOH and print reach
  - market context (sales reuse)
- **Custom only:**
  - the sponsor's objectives
  - committed vs delivered
  - share of voice
  - the named roster and geography
  - the narrative
- **Missing in both, worth adding:**
  - feedback
  - delivered status with evidence
  - for Custom, wishlist met and follow-ups
- **Wishlist met for IP** is a per-partner add-on page, only for partners who bought Pitch Room, Leadership Dialogues or Customer Success Showcase. It is not part of the event-level IP report.
