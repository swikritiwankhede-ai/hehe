How the post-event deck gets made

From the first target entered before the event to the deck in sponsors’ inboxes by 12:00 the day after, and the refreshed v2 two weeks later. Teams capture data in the portal as they work; the platform does the assembling, checking and slide-building.

What the platform does with the data

Every number is computed once in the metrics registry. The report page and the PPT both read from it, so they can never disagree.

Rejected paths loop back: failed checks go to the Review queue or the source module; a not-ready section is dropped or included with a logged reason.

Rules the flow enforces

Capture on the day

Check-in, session-tagged photos and audio, and every social post URL are recorded during the event. The readiness checklist names the owner of anything missing from T-7 onwards.

One number, one definition

Each metric carries its formula, base (n), source and “as of” time. Social reach uses the agreed windows: pre-event = post date → event start; during = event days.

AI drafts, people approve

AI suggests classifications, verbatim quotes from speaker audio, photo picks and one-line takeaways. It never writes a number, and nothing reaches a slide without approval.

Totals only leave the portal

Names, emails and attendee lists stay in the internal page. The deck shows aggregates and organisation names, and every export is frozen and logged.
